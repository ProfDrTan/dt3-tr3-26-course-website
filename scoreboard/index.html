How the board is checked

Only three places count: the team repository’s main branch, the team’s own sections on the shared FigJam board, and the team’s Discord channel. Work on other branches, in other boards or in other chats is not seen.

Every “Team N - …” section on the FigJam board is read, not only the newest one. Arrival times come from GitHub’s server-side record of when work reached main, not from commit dates. Each update is a snapshot taken at the time shown at the top, and every evidence link points to the exact commit and FigJam section that was checked.

Scrum light: 🟢 Following Scrum = every check passes · 🟠 Drifting from Scrum = one check fails · 🔴 Not following Scrum = two or more checks fail, or a missed prediction lock, or no GitHub commit for 3 or more days.

Checks: Sprint Backlog visible (Sprint 9 cards on FigJam) · Daily Scrum (at least 10 messages in the team Discord channel in the last 48 hours) · Increment on main (team work reaching main during Sprint 9) · Sprint timebox kept (every milestone due so far on time) · Whole team at the Sprint Review (at most one absent at the latest practical) · Whole team involved this week (at least half the team active on GitHub or Discord).
