The Convention — Read This First

Sprint N = Week N of teaching. Always. No exceptions, no offsets.

The tag you'll see on every sprint (vW1, vW2, ... vW9) is just the sprint number. Not a calendar week number, not an ISO week — the same number, every time. If it's Sprint 4, the tag is vW4. That's the whole rule.

Study Week gets no number and no tag.

It's skipped in the sequence entirely — you won't see a "Sprint 5.5" or a gap tag. Sprint 5 is followed by Study Week (unnumbered), then Sprint 6 picks up where the count left off.

The last numbered sprint is Sprint 9 — not Demo Day.

Demo Day is the Monday after Sprint 9 closes. It has no sprint number and no tag. Nothing new is predicted or submitted that week — it's presentation only.
