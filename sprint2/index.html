Sprint 2 — vW2 — What Must You Do

Your team formed today. This is what happens between now and next Monday, and what you'll present when you come back.

The Sprint Goal

Produce and present a manual market intelligence snapshot as a team — no automation, no scripts, just done by hand.

This is deliberate. You'll feel exactly where the manual work gets repetitive and painful — that feeling is the reason automation gets introduced next, and it'll make far more sense once you've felt the problem it solves.

The Backlog — Four Things, By Hand

1SPX close for the week

Find and record the S&P 500 index close for each trading day this week. Note the weekly change.

DONE MEANS — a written record of 5 daily closes plus the net weekly move, with the source noted.
2One sector's performance

Pick one market sector (tech, energy, financials, whatever your team chooses) and track how it moved this week relative to the broader market.

DONE MEANS — which sector, its move, and one sentence on whether it beat or lagged the index.
3One macro data point

Find one piece of macroeconomic news or data released this week (Fed commentary, inflation data, jobs numbers, anything market-relevant) and note what it was and how markets reacted.

DONE MEANS — the data point, the date, and the market's reaction in a sentence or two.
4One calendar/seasonality note

Is there anything seasonal or calendar-driven relevant to this week (earnings season, a known historical pattern, an upcoming event)? Note it, even briefly.

DONE MEANS — one observation, with reasoning for why it's relevant.

Then: Write It Up and Present

Bring a short write-up of all four to Monday's session. Every team member should be able to speak to at least one part of it — this isn't a solo effort with everyone else riding along.

What Monday Actually Is

Two things happen in the same session: a Sprint Review of what you just did by hand (what you found, what was painful, what took too long), and Sprint 2 Planning for what comes next. The second half is introduced live — come having done the work, not having guessed what's coming.
