New: Your Track Record (track-record/vW6.json)

Owner: R4, with R2, R3 and R6. Due Saturday 17 Oct, 23:59. One file with two parts.

Part 1 — Every locked call (R4)

One row for every call your team locked from Sprint 3 to Sprint 5, using your own predictions/ and actuals/ files. Each row has the sprint, the market, your call, the actual close, and the miss:

S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100: % miss = |P − A| ÷ A × 100
where P is your predicted close, A is the actual close, and |…| means the size of the miss, whether you were above or below.

10-year yield: miss in basis points = |P − A| × 100
where P is your predicted yield and A is the actual yield, both in percent. One basis point is 0.01 percentage points, so 4.27 against 4.31 is a 4 basis-point miss.

Gold: right or wrong direction.

A call committed after its lock goes in with "late": true and is left out of your averages. A sprint where your team locked no call is recorded as "no call". Don't fill it in after the fact.

Part 2 — Which evidence pointed the right way (R2, R3, R6)

For each sprint you have records for, each of these roles writes which way their own evidence pointed (up or down) for each market they covered, and which way the market actually went:

  • R2: almanac and seasonal evidence, and macro evidence, as two separate entries.
  • R3: technical evidence.
  • R6: each of ChatGPT, Gemini and DeepSeek separately.

Use only what is already committed in your repo for that sprint. If the evidence for a sprint wasn't committed, record it as "not recorded". That is a finding, not a gap to fill.
