Sprint 8 — vW8 — Catch Up, Then Read the Volume

Every team finishes a gold page that passes the live test. Then every team adds the one thing a price-only page cannot tell you: how much conviction is behind the move.

This sprint has two steps, in order. Step A — finish Sprint 7: your ui/gold.html passes all five Sprint 7 rules. Step B — add volume: a Price & Volume panel on the same page (section 3). Teams that passed Monday’s live test start at Step B on day one. Teams that did not pass do Step A first; a reference engine is available to help (section 2). Nobody is left behind, and nobody gets credit for Step B while Step A still fails.
Why. By Saturday, the Sprint 7 repos showed most teams close but not finished: needles pointing the wrong way, a Summary built from only some of the votes, missing team pages, files appearing on Saturday night. This sprint gives everyone the time to close those gaps — and the Wednesday checkpoint is now checked automatically from GitHub, so the work has to happen during the week, not at the end.

1. Where each team starts

Every team starts at Step A and checks itself against the eight checks in section 2. A team that passes all eight at Monday 26 Oct's live test starts at Step B straight away.

3. Step B — add a Price & Volume panel

Every rating on your page is built from price. Volume tells you whether real money is behind the move. Add this panel to ui/gold.html, directly under the bar time, so it changes with the selected tile. This is what it must look like:

PartExactly what it shows
CandlesThe last 60 completed bars of the selected timeframe (green up, red down)
Volume barsUnder the candles, one bar per candle, same colour as its candle
Average lineDashed line: the average volume of the 20 bars before each bar
Time axisTimes in SGT along the bottom (dates for 4 Hours and Daily)
Relative volumelast completed bar’s volume ÷ average of the 20 bars before it, shown as e.g. “1.62×”
Volume labelOne of four, by this rule (same for every team):
Confirmed — relative volume ≥ 1.5 and the last bar moved in the Summary’s direction
Warning — relative volume ≥ 1.5 and the last bar moved against the Summary
Thin — relative volume < 0.7 (low conviction)
Normal — anything else
  • Data: Yahoo already gives you volume in the same download (Volume column). For 4 Hours and Daily, add up the volume of the 1-hour bars you combine.
  • Write it into data.json per timeframe: the 60 bars (time, open, high, low, close, volume), the 20-bar average, relative volume and label. The page only reads data.json.
  • Gotcha: the last bar of the week (Friday night) or a bar still forming has partial volume. Use completed bars only, and say so on the page.
  • Verify: add one line per timeframe to verify.md: your volume for one bar vs the volume shown for the same bar on Investing.com’s gold chart.
Stretch (optional, for teams that finish early): a chart of how the Summary score moved over the last 60 bars, drawn as a smooth line across shaded Strong Sell → Strong Buy bands.
