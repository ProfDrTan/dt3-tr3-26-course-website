Sprint 9, Part 1: How wrong were we?

Sprint 9 comes in two parts. Part 1 (this brief) runs from Mon 2 Nov to Sat 7 Nov. Part 2 will be released over the weekend of 7–8 Nov, based on where each team stands after Part 1. Mon 9 Nov is a public holiday, so there is no presentation between the two parts. Instead, your progress is shown on the new Scrum Scoreboard, linked from the course home page from Thu 5 Nov.

Part 1 goal: by Saturday, every team can show, with real numbers, how accurate its past predictions were, and has written down what it will change. In Part 2 you will put that change to the test.

The idea in one minute

Every week since Sprint 4 your team has locked a prediction for where SPX, NDX, gold and the 10-year yield (TNX) would close on Friday. Those predictions are sitting in your repo, and nobody has checked them yet.

Think of a weather forecaster. Making forecasts is only half the job. The other half is looking back: "I said 30 degrees, it was 26. I keep forecasting too hot. Why? What do I change?" A forecaster who never looks back never improves.

1. Sprint 9 backlog (FigJam)

In your team's section of the shared board, add one card for each of milestones 2 to 6. Each card names its owner and gives one line saying what "done" means. Move the cards as the work progresses. Use the shared board only; copies of the board don't count.

2. vW9 prediction

From now on, every team writes its prediction in exactly this format: numbers only, no % signs, and no actual closes in the file.

predictions/vW9.json
{
  "week": "vW9",
  "target_date": "2026-11-06",
  "SPX": 7600,
  "NDX": 28500,
  "GOLD": 3920,
  "TNX": 4.95
}

GOLD means the December 2026 gold future. TNX is the yield in percent (4.95 means 4.95%).

3. Old predictions in one table

Copy your vW4 to vW8 numbers from your old prediction files into one table. Don't edit the old files themselves.

data/old_predictions.csv
week,target_date,SPX,NDX,GOLD,TNX,source_file
vW8,2026-10-30,7750,29800,4150,4.99,predictions/vW8.json

If a week had no number for an instrument, leave that cell blank. For example, gold was a direction call before vW6, and TNX started in vW5.

4. Error table, made by a script

Write engine/check_error.py. It reads old_predictions.csv and vW9.json, downloads each actual Friday close from Yahoo Finance, and writes data/errors.csv:

week,target_date,instrument,predicted,actual,error,unit
vW8,2026-10-30,SPX,7750,7580.07,2.24,pct
vW8,2026-10-30,TNX,4.99,4.929,6.1,bp
  • Yahoo symbols: ^GSPC (SPX), ^NDX, GCZ26.CMX (gold), ^TNX. Yahoo is the only source, so every team gets the same actual closes.
  • Error = (predicted − actual) ÷ actual × 100 for SPX, NDX and gold. For TNX, error = (predicted − actual) × 100, in basis points. A plus sign means you predicted too high.
  • Your vW9 row fills in once Friday 6 Nov's close is available, so run the script again on Saturday.

5. Error charts

On your team page, show one chart per instrument: the error each week, with a line at zero and the Friday dates along the bottom. Above the line means too high; below means too low. One look should show whether your team keeps making the same mistake.

6. What went wrong

In docs/what_went_wrong.md, each instrument's owner writes one short paragraph covering three points:

  • What we see: quote the weeks from your error table (for example, "too high in vW6, vW7 and vW8").
  • Why we think it happened: which part of our reasoning misled us.
  • What we will change: one specific change, not "be more careful".

How results are shown

  • Only main counts. Work on another branch, on a laptop, in a chat or on a hosting site doesn't count. Every team's main is checked every hour, so the time each file arrived is recorded automatically.
  • Each milestone shows one result: ✅ On time, ⚠️ Late (with the time it arrived), or ❌ No submission. A result is fixed at its deadline and is not changed afterwards.
  • Your work appears on the scoreboard: your numbers, your table, a picture of your charts and your paragraphs. Predictions appear only after their lock time has passed.
  • When the scoreboard updates: Thursday 5 Nov for milestones 1–3, and Sunday 8 Nov for milestones 4–6. Part 2 is shaped by these results.
  • The scoreboard also shows whether your Daily Scrum is happening in your Discord team channel.
